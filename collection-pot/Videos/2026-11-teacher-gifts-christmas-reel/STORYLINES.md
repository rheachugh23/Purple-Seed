## B. "Not another mug" (the bold one)

**Territory:** 3. Poor gift giving

**The idea:** one long, slow camera move along a teacher's desk on the last day of term, buried in mugs, candles and chocolate tins. Deadpan numbers count up the pile until the joke lands. Then a hard cut to Gold, where the class picks one thing together.

| Timing | What happens on the screen | VO | On screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.8s | **[Higgsfield]** One continuous slow dolly begins along a teacher's desk on the last day of term. The desk is piled with mugs, candles and chocolate tins. Warm classroom light, no people. | | **30 kids.** |
| 1.8–3.6s | The same take continues along the pile. | | **30 presents.** |
| 3.6–5.4s | The dolly passes a tower of mugs. | | **11 mugs.** |
| 5.4–7.5s | The dolly ends on a wilting poinsettia. | | **1 teacher who'd rather choose.** |
| 7.5–10.5s | Hard cut to Gold. Chip with a stack of presents (`137 - Gifts`). | | **They pick the gift. You all get the credit.** |
| 10.5–13.5s | Chip shares the Pot (`24 - Share a Pot 2`), and the link flies off as a paper plane. | | **One Pot. One link. Everyone chips in.** |
| 13.5–17.5s | A **NEW** sticker pops on, and plain gift cards fan out (Pot icon only, no shop logos). | | **New: add a Gift Card** · *A shop they love, or a multi-store card.* |
| 17.5–21.0s | The Pot splits in two: part becomes a gift box, and the rest becomes a gift card. | | **Mix and match.** · *Buy a gift, put the rest on a Gift Card.* |
| 21.0–24.5s | The real printed No.1 Teacher card drops in. | | **No trip to the shops.** · *Print it at home, or we'll post it.* |
| 24.5–30.0s | **End card on Gold:** Chip winks (`65 - Winking right`), and the Pot pops into the logo. | | **Teacher gifts made effortless** · button: **CREATE A POT** |

The numbers are a joke, not data.

**Sound:** a deadpan plucked bass line. Each number lands with a ceramic mug "clink", and the clinks get louder. The music lifts on the cut to Gold.

**Watch-out:** the joke has to feel affectionate, never like it's mocking the kids' presents. It's the riskiest of the three and the most shareable.

**Higgsfield shots (1):** the continuous desk dolly (8 s, 9:16).

## C. "Last day of term"

**Territory:** 2. Gifting is better when done together

**The idea:** told from the receiver's side. It's 3:31pm on the last day of term and the classroom is empty. A phone lights up on the teacher's desk, and every family's message pours out of it. Then we show how simple it was to make: one Pot, with the gift inside.

(Daniel) · "Your guidance and kindness mean so much 💛" (Sofia) |

- A warm piano and glockenspiel bed builds as the messages arrive, with a soft pop on each one.
- It settles under the end card.
