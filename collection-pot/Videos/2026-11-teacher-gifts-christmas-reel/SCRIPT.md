## A. "The class chat" (recommended)

**Why this one:** it's the email's own story, and every parent knows the situation. One phone carries the whole film, from the chat chaos to the teacher's desk.

**Device and form:** life before and after, told inside one group chat. Each shot ends on the thing that opens the next: the chat becomes the Pot link, the link becomes Chip's phone, the Pot becomes a printed card, and the card lands on the teacher's desk.

**What you see and feel:** a phone buzzing itself off the kitchen table as the class chat melts down over the teacher's present. Then one gold message drops in, the chat goes quiet, and everything calm after it is Chip's world.

**First two seconds, exactly:** night, a kitchen table, fairy lights out of focus. A phone lies face up and lights up, then lights up again. Big type is already on screen at frame 0: **"What do we get for the teacher?"**

**Beats:** hook (phone buzzing) → chat chaos → one gold Pot link → Chip → messages pile in → NEW Gift Cards → no trip to the shops → the teacher's desk → end card.

**Look:**
- The old way is a real home at night (Higgsfield): warm tungsten light, shallow focus, fairy-light bokeh.
- The new way is a flat Pot of Gold stage with 3D Chip.
- Type is Faro Display Lucky for headlines and Inter for chat and UI.
- On Gold, bubbles and cards are White and Black only. No secondary colours sit on Gold.

**Sound:**
- The chaos plays almost silent: just phone buzzes and notification pops that speed up.
- The music opens when the gold Pot link lands: light and festive, plucked strings or glockenspiel, around 110 bpm.
- Pops play on each message and on the NEW sticker.
- One riser leads into the logo.

## B. "Not another mug" (the bold one)

**Device and form:** a number lands, as giant kinetic type over **one continuous take**.

**What you see and feel:** one long, slow camera move along a teacher's desk on the last day of term, buried in mugs, candles and chocolate tins. Deadpan numbers count up the pile until the joke lands. Then a hard cut to Gold, where the class picks one thing together.

**First two seconds, exactly:** a slow push along a cluttered desk of mugs in warm classroom light, no people. **"30 kids."** is already on screen in huge type.

**Beats:** 30 kids → 30 presents → 11 mugs → 1 teacher who'd rather choose → hard cut to Gold → "They pick the gift. You all get the credit." → one Pot, one link → NEW Gift Cards → mix and match → print at home → end card.
