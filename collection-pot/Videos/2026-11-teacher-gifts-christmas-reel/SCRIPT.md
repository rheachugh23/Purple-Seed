# Script: "Last day of term"

**Status:** locked 26 Nov 2026 (storyline C). The other options are in `STORYLINES.md`.
**Territory:** 2. Gifting is better when done together
**Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30 s. Music, sound effects and a voiceover (added 27 Nov; the music-only cut is kept too).
**Campaign line:** "Teacher's gift made easy". It lands on the end card with the emailer's hero Chip, so the Reel and the emailer read as one campaign.
**Idea:** told from the receiver's side. It's 3:31pm on the last day of term, and a phone lights up on the teacher's desk in an empty classroom. Every family's message pours out of it. Then we show how simple it was to make: one Pot, with the gift inside.

**Voiceover (added 27 Nov 2026):**
- **Voice:** "Elizabeth", a warm British female narrator from ElevenLabs's own voice set (eleven_v4 model).
- **Recording:** one take of all seven lines, cut at its pauses and placed on the beats. The times in brackets are when each line starts.
- **Copy:** the VO adds to the on-screen words rather than reading them out. It names the families, pokes fun at chasing money, and says the campaign line, "Teacher's gift made easy", on the end card.
- **Mix:** the music dips under every line (about −8 dB, and −10 dB on the end card) and is carved in the voice's frequencies. It comes back up in the gaps: the cut to Gold at 12.5s, 15.6–17.4s and 19.5–20.9s.

**Sound:**
- Quiet room tone and a distant school bell at the start.
- A warm piano and glockenspiel bed builds as the messages arrive, with a soft pop on each card.
- The bed opens up on the cut to Gold. One riser leads into the logo, and it settles under the end card.

**Higgsfield plates (2):**
1. The empty-classroom push-in (5 s).
2. The desk from above, as one locked-off take with the phone held still: hands come in, rest, then clasp (12 s).

**Look (storyboard v3, 26 Nov):**

**Copy changes from the storyline draft:**
- 24.0s: the end card now leads with the campaign line, **"Teacher's gift made easy."**, to match the emailer. "A card and gift in one, from everyone." moves underneath as the subline, and that beat grows by 0.5 s (the film is now 30 s).
- 2.5s: "One Pot. From the whole class." became **"From the whole class."**, so "One Pot" isn't said twice.
- 12.5s: shortened to fit a 4.5-second read. The headline is now **"Now the gift's sorted too."**, the Gift Card line moved onto the NEW sticker, and the subline is now "A shop they love, or a multi-store card."
- 17.0s: "Everyone chips in." became a subline under **"One Pot. One link."**, because it didn't fit as a third headline line.
- 24.0s: the end card is now two beats (the claim, then the logo and button), so the logo and CREATE A POT sit clear of Instagram's caption and buttons.
