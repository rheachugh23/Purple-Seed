# Purple Seed

Video and marketing work for brands. Each brand has its own workspace folder (e.g. `collection-pot/`).

## Collection Pot

Read `collection-pot/brand/BRAND.md` before making anything. Videos live in `collection-pot/Videos/<YYYY-MM-slug>/`, one folder per video (see `collection-pot/Videos/README.md`).

### Scripts: always this format
Every script is a table with these four columns, in this order. Leave a cell empty when the script doesn't need it (e.g. no VO).

| Timing | What happens on the screen | VO | On screen text |
|---|---|---|---|

Sound, music and production notes go under the table, not in extra columns.

### Communication territories
Every script or storyline names its territory at the top. When offering options, spread them across different territories. Details are in `collection-pot/brand/BRAND.md` → "Communication territories".
1. Awkwardness of a gift organiser
2. Gifting is better when done together (meaningful gifting, joy of the receiver)
3. Poor gift giving
4. Post brown envelope era
5. Cultural moments (e.g. GTA 6 launch, The Traitors, Dune 3, Avengers)

### Look and feel (feedback, Nov 2026)
- **Not too yellow.** Never a frame that's all Gold. Mix Wedding White, Gold and a touch of Work Bash Black, divided by big crops of the Pot shape (brand pp.42–44).
- **The Pot shape** comes from `collection-pot/brand/references/website/site-assets/Marketing Website/Icons/potYellow.svg`. Recolour it (CSS mask) or reuse its path for outlines, but never redraw it. Use it as background crops, punctuation (a Pot full stop), shapes around Chip, and seasonal props (e.g. baubles, snow).
- **Playful type** (brand p.50): standard Faro headlines plus "expressive" bouncing letters on 1–2 key words, with a small standfirst above.
- **Seasonal pieces must feel seasonal.** Use Chip's matching renders (e.g. the Santa-hat renders for Christmas) and seasonal props built from the Pot shape.

### Hard rules
- **No people's faces** in any video. Hands, objects and places only.
- **Never recreate a brand asset** (logo, Chip, the Pot icon, printed cards, fonts). Always use the files in `collection-pot/brand/` as they are. Chip is never AI-generated, re-drawn or animated by an AI video model.
- Higgsfield is for live-action plates only (rooms, desks, hands, phones). Never Chip, the logo or card artwork.
- Workflow for a video: script → storyboard → final video, with the person's go between each step.
