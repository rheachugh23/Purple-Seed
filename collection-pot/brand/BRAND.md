# Collection Pot: brand essentials

A condensed version of `guidelines/Collection_Pot_Brand_Guidelines_Nov2026.pdf` (63 pp). Page numbers point back to the PDF.

## 1. Strategy (pp.4–8)
- **Brand promise:** *Easy to sort. Impossible to forget.*
  - *Easy to sort* is fronted by **Chip**, the group celebration assistant.
  - *Impossible to forget* is receiving the Pot: a digital card and gift in one, full of everyone's messages.
- **Internal brand idea:** "Chip in, chill out". **Never use it in customer comms.**
- **What:** The easiest way to sort a group gift, for any occasion.
- **How:** One little Pot. One helpful link that brings everyone together. Chip collects the money and messages, and the Pot arrives as a digital card and gift in one, to spend on whatever they like.
- **Why:** Group gifts mark life's big moments, but sorting them is a job nobody wants. Chasing for money, giving out bank details, presents nobody really chose. So. Much. Stress.
- **Purpose:** We believe life is better, and more fun, when we do it together with a smile.
- **Personality:** Personal · Thoughtful · Quick
- **Story cast** (made-up relatable characters): Dan from Finance's leaving prezzie, Aunt Su's big 50th surprise, Sam and Chris' wedding gift.

### Communication territories
Every piece of comms sits in one of these five territories (set by the Collection Pot team, Nov 2026; not in the PDF). Name the territory at the top of every script or storyline. When offering options, spread them across different territories.

| # | Territory | Example angles (draft, not from the team) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Awkwardness of a gift organiser** | Chasing money, reminder messages, sharing bank details, the group chat, being the one who has to sort it |
| 2 | **Gifting is better when done together** | Meaningful gifting, the joy of the receiver, everyone's messages in one place |
| 3 | **Poor gift giving** | Gifts nobody really chose: the 12th mug, the last-minute petrol-station present |
| 4 | **Post brown envelope era** | The old way: the brown envelope of cash passed round the office or the class (copy bank: "In our post-envelope era.") |
| 5 | **Cultural moments** | Riding big moments in culture, e.g. the GTA 6 launch, The Traitors, Dune 3, Avengers. Nod to them; never use their logos, characters, footage or music |

## 2. Tone of voice (pp.10–17)
| Trait | Means | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | The organiser's best friend. Friendly, conversational, direct. | Write like you talk. Use made-up names (Aunt Su). Colloquialisms people know (DMs, leaving do), but don't overuse them. |
| **Thoughtful** | We think about every part of group gifting so you don't have to. | Put yourself in the organiser's shoes. Ask thoughtful questions. Solve real pain points (chasing money). |
| **Quick** (literally, and witted) | Collection pain points aren't fun, but they can be funny. | Get to the point. Short, punchy sentences. Twist common phrases. Poke fun at the pain points. |

**We would say:** Chip in together · Collect with the people you know, for the people you know · Collection Pot / Pots · Because a bank transfer doesn't really say 'I've thought about you', does it…
**We wouldn't say:** Collective gift money · Group collections for your connections · Group pots / Money pools · Get more thoughtful gifts here!!!

**Owning our name (p.15):** always capitalise the **P** in Pot / Pots / Collection Pot, including when it's used as a noun ("set up a Pot"). The website uses Collection Pot™.
> Whoever you're collecting for. Whatever you're collecting for. We've got a Pot for every occasion.

## 3. Logo (pp.19–24)
- **Stacked** and **Linear** wordmarks, with the Pot as the counter of the 'O'. Neither takes priority: pick whichever fits the layout.
- **Approved colour pairs only:** Black on White · White on Black · Gold on Black · Black on Gold · Gold on White.
- **Clear space:** stacked = width of the Pot counter; linear = 2× the Pot counter.
- **Minimum size:** stacked 180 px / 30 mm; linear 300 px / 50 mm.
- **Motion:** the Pot leads. In the short version the Pot pops into the 'O'. In the standard version it bounces, flips once, and lands in the 'O', then the letters reveal from behind it. Calm and intentional: no scattering letters, shines, stretching, or erratic moves.
- Files: `logos/` (linear black, linear white, Pot-icon lockup). A stacked-logo SVG isn't in the repo yet; ask the brand team.

## 4. Chip the Zenguin (pp.25–41)
Cool, calm and collected. Uses he/him in the guidelines. Built almost entirely from Pot shapes.
- **Is:** quick-witted, friendly, cool, calm, collected, thoughtful. **Isn't:** aggressive, flappable, stressed, irritating, unbothered.
- **Voice:** rarely speaks. Short and sharp. Greets, guides and signs off ads, but is never the full voice of the brand.
  Would say: "That's everyone in. Nice work." · "Pot delivered. Cue the happy tears." · "Off to a flying start! Well, you are at least…" · "We'll chip away at this together."
  Wouldn't say: "Follow us on social media now!!!!" · "We do some egg-cellent time-saving!" (no penguin puns)
- **Drawing rules:** only the eyes and beak change. Leans follow the Pot angle (27°, or 10° for a slight lean). Feet are detached when still, one attached when waddling, both touching the body when leaning. Shadows must be consistent.
- **Scenes:** 2–3 accessories at most, always smaller than Chip, brand colours only, tonal where possible, using the Pot shape where possible.
- **Motion:** friendly and social, calm and confident, occasionally witty. Idle loops are seamless (gentle sway or nod). No stretching, harsh movement or over-excitement.

### 2D vs 3D Chip (p.35)
| 2D Chip: everyday | 3D Chip: hero moments |
|---|---|
| App and website, icons and avatars, illustration scenes, everyday social and print | Film and animation, hero social and campaigns, posters and big moments, merch |

**Never mix 2D and 3D Chip in the same piece.**

### 3D Chip rules (pp.36–41)
- One locked, rigged master model. **Never AI-generated or off-model variations.** Use the renders in `mascot-3d/renders/`.
- Soft, matte, clay-like finish with gentle studio light. Black body, White face and belly, Gold beak and feet. Double quiff, Pot-shaped eyes.
- Expressions: **Happy** (the big moment, when the Pot lands) · **Neutral** (listening, helping, idling) · **Wink** (signature sign-off) · **Sad** (only for the old way of collecting, never at the payoff).
- Poses: phone (sharing the link), gift (the Pot as the gift), confetti (big moment), party popper, deckchair (job done), sign (holding a message).
- **Backgrounds:** Pot of Gold or Wedding White only. **Never on Work Bash Black.**
- In live action, animate him in after filming, lit to match the scene, with a real shadow.
- He doesn't talk: he acts, reacts and signs off with a wink.

## 5. The Pot device (pp.42–45)
The Pot shape is the graphic system. It's the 'O' in the logo, a crop or background divider, punctuation on key words, the base of every icon, a prop for Chip, and the avatar and favicon.

## 7. Typography (pp.49–54) → `fonts/`
- **Headlines: Faro Display Lucky**, sentence case, optical kerning, tracking −10, leading 100%. *Expressive* setting (tilted, bouncing letters) is for 1–2 words only.
- **Standfirsts:** Faro Display Lucky, UPPERCASE.
- **Subheadings:** Inter SemiBold, sentence case, tracking −25, leading 110%.
- **Body:** Inter Regular, sentence case, tracking −25, leading 120%.
- **Buttons and stickers:** Inter SemiBold, UPPERCASE, tracking +25, leading 100% (CREATE A POT, CHAT TO US, VIEW MESSAGES).
- **Text motion:** hold text at least 2 s; for longer copy, allow about 1.5 s per line. Styles: "Max relaxing" (letters slide in, squish, then relax into a lean; for under 5 words), "Chip slide in" (letters bounce up in Chip's trail), and "Longer text" (line by line from below the baseline).
- Faro Display Lucky is a commercial font (Displaay). The `.otf` here was pulled from the site's CDN, so check the licence covers your use. Inter is free (Google Fonts).

## 8. Icons (p.55)
Functional icons feature the Pot device. Use them as icons, never as hero illustrations. The files in `icons/` are the site's current UI and occasion icons, and not all of them follow the Pot style.
