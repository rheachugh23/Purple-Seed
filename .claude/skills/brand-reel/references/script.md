# Storylines and the script

## Storylines (`STORYLINES.md`)

Write three, because a first idea alone is usually the obvious one:

- **Different films.** Each has its own world (where it happens), its own device (what the story is) and its own form (how the camera and cuts carry it).
- **Different territories.** If the brand has communication territories, give each storyline a different one and name it at the top.
- **One bold option.** One of the three is the one you wouldn't have picked first.
- **A recommendation** comes first, with the reason in one clause.

For each storyline, write:
- its name, territory, device and form, and in two sentences what the viewer sees and feels;
- the first two seconds, exactly (frame 0 is the thumbnail, so it's never a logo or a blank frame);
- the beats in one line;
- the look in one line, drawn from the mood card and the brand;
- the sound in one line;
- which shots need Higgsfield plates, and roughly how many seconds.

Show the three and stop until the person picks. A mix ("A's opening with C's ending") counts as a pick.

## The locked script (`SCRIPT.md`)

Use this layout, in this order, every time:

```markdown
# Script: "<storyline name>"

**Status:** locked <date>.
**Territory:** <n. name>
**Campaign line:** <the line shared with the rest of the campaign>
**Format:** <platform>, 1080×1920, 30 fps, <length> s. <VO or no VO; music; SFX>.

| Timing | What happens on the screen | VO | On screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5s | **[Higgsfield 1]** … | | **…** |
| 2.5–5.5s | **[Higgsfield 2]** … | | **…** |
| 12.5–17.0s | Brand frame: … (asset file names) | | **…** · *subline* |

**Sound:** …
**Higgsfield plates (N):** what each plate is, its length, and which rows it serves.
**Copy changes from the storyline draft:** any wording you changed, and why.
```

- **Timing** in seconds, back to back, matching the cut.
- **What happens on the screen** says what the viewer sees. Mark AI shots `[Higgsfield N]` and brand frames with the asset files they use.
- **VO** holds the spoken words exactly, with the speaker name if there's more than one. Leave it empty when there's no VO.
- **On screen text** holds the exact words, with headline and subline separated by `·`.
- Sound and production notes go under the table, never in extra columns.

## Copy rules

- The client's copy is used verbatim. If a line must change to fit the timing, list the change under the table.
- Follow the brand's casing and naming rules (e.g. a capitalised product noun).
- Keep a line to about six words, cutting words rather than shrinking type. Give it about 0.5 s plus 0.3 s per word on screen, and at least 2 s for anything that must be read. The last line holds at least 2 s.
- With VO, the screen doesn't repeat the spoken sentence word for word.
- Without VO, the titles carry the story. Reels autoplay muted, so the pictures plus the words must tell it alone.
- Carry the campaign line (e.g. on the end card) so the video and the rest of the campaign read as one.
