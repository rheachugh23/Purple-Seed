# Teacher gifts: Christmas Reel

| | |
|---|---|
| **Brief** | Turn the "Teacher gifts made effortless" emailer into an Instagram Reel for Christmas class gifting. |
| **Source** | `source-email.md` (the email's copy, verbatim) |
| **Channel** | Instagram Reels |
| **Format** | 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30 s, music, sound effects and a voiceover (a music-only cut is kept too) |
| **Campaign line** | Teacher's gift made easy (in sync with the emailer) |
| **Tools** | Higgsfield (live-action plates, no faces, and the music) + ElevenLabs (voiceover) + HyperFrames (type, Chip, logo, edit, render) |
| **Chip** | 3D only (no 2D Chip): renders 121 With gift and 139 Wink left, each with a Santa-hat layer (hat lifted from render 108), plus the two Christmas emailer images (cut out) |
| **Territory** | 2. Gifting is better when done together |
| **Status** | Step 3: final film with voiceover (v2), `export/teacher-gifts-christmas-reel-vo_1080x1920.mp4`. Music-only v1: `export/teacher-gifts-christmas-reel_1080x1920.mp4` |

## Files
- `SCRIPT.md`: the locked script, "Last day of term" (territory 2), in the four-column format
- `STORYLINES.md`: all three storyline options (A, B, C), plus the Instagram caption draft
- `higgsfield/PROMPTS.md`: the two Higgsfield plate prompts and settings. Generated clips go in `higgsfield/` as well.
- `project/`: the HyperFrames project
  - `STORYBOARD.md`: the storyboard plan, 9 frames
  - `boards/storyboard.html`: the storyboard page, with stills in `boards/stills/`
  - `SPEC.md`: the director's brief (look, motion and shots, with numbers)
  - `index.html` + `compositions/`: one scene per frame
  - `assets/`: copies of the brand files used (Chip renders, logo, the Pot shape, printed card, fonts), plus the Teacher Pot screen captures
- `project/assets/audio/vo/`: the voiceover. `vo-take.mp3` is the ElevenLabs take as delivered, and `vo-01.wav`–`vo-07.wav` are its seven lines, levelled and cut (`VO.md` has the settings).
- `export/`: the renders. `teacher-gifts-christmas-reel-vo_1080x1920.mp4` has the voiceover (v2), and `teacher-gifts-christmas-reel_1080x1920.mp4` is music only (v1).

Storyboard page (private): https://claude.ai/artifact/7Xia8LDydtmUUGVQCLVDoA

## Rules for this piece
- No people's faces anywhere. Live-action shots show hands, objects and places only.
- Chip, the logo, the Pot icon and the printed card come from `../../brand/` unchanged. They're never recreated or AI-generated.
