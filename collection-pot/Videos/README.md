# Videos

Every Collection Pot video gets its **own folder** here. Static marketing assets (posts, posters, carousels) stay in `../assets/`.

```
Videos/
└── YYYY-MM-<short-slug>/          e.g. 2026-11-teacher-gifts-christmas-reel
    ├── README.md                  brief, channel, format, status, what each file is
    ├── source-email.md            (or source-*.md) the brief or source copy, verbatim
    ├── SCRIPT.md                  step 1: script (storyline options, then the picked one)
    ├── STORYBOARD.md              step 2: storyboard, one frame per shot
    ├── higgsfield/                Higgsfield prompts (PROMPTS.md) and the clips they made
    ├── project/                   step 3: the HyperFrames project (index.html, compositions/, assets/)
    └── export/                    final renders, named <slug>_<width>x<height>.mp4
```

## How we make a video

1. **Script.** Three storylines in words, pick one, then lock the copy.
2. **Storyboard.** One still per shot with its timing, words and sound. Approve it before animating.
3. **Final video.** Higgsfield makes the live-action plates; HyperFrames builds and renders the film.

## Rules for every video

- **No people's faces.** Live-action shots show hands, objects and places only.
- **Never recreate brand assets.** Chip, the logo, the Pot icon, printed cards and fonts come from `../brand/` as they are. Chip is never AI-generated, re-drawn or animated by an AI model. He moves only as the official renders, with simple moves (pop, slide, gentle sway).
- **Higgsfield is for live-action plates only:** rooms, desks, hands, phones, weather. Never Chip, the logo or the card artwork.
- **3D Chip** stands on Pot of Gold or Wedding White only, never on Work Bash Black, and never in the same piece as 2D Chip.
- Copy follows `../brand/BRAND.md`: capital P in Pot, short punchy lines, and headlines held on screen for at least 2 s.
- Instagram Reels: 1080×1920 at 30 fps. Keep words inside the safe zone (288–1248 px from the top).
