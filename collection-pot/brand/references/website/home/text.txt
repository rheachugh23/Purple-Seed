How spending a Pot works

As the Pot owner, you’re free to cash it out with no fees, spend it on our products, or send it as an e-gift. If you choose the e-gift option, the recipient can swap it for 60+ big-name gift cards.

Here’s what you can do with a Pot:

Take as cash – instant transfer to your own bank account

Gift cards – buy physical gift cards (securely posted)

Printed cards – turn your e-card into physical card

Flowers or hampers – order gifts direct and pay from the Pot

Send as e-gift – recipients can spend the Pot at 60+ retailers
